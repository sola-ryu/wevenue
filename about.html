our little story~

It started with a question: "what if you could just tell an AI what you want, and it would make it happen?" Not drag-and-drop. Not templates. Just... you describe it, we build it. uwu~

We gathered a team of specialized AI agents — a designer who loves pretty colors, a developer who writes clean code, and a QA agent who's basically a professional bug hunter. Together, they became the Wevenue team~ 🎀
